# L28 Emergency in pediatric surgery (Chatdow Choungboonsri, M.D.) — main (ไม่มี suffix = สไลด์ปีนี้ ไม่มีลายมือ) · 33 หน้า · วงกลมสีในหัวสไลด์เป็นสีธีม ไม่ใช่สัญลักษณ์คณะ
p1 ปก · p2/p3 agenda: abdominal wall defect, acute abdomen in children, acute scrotum
p4 รูป omphalocele (sac + cord) vs gastroschisis (bowel ไม่มี sac)
p5–7 ตาราง (build-up) Omphalocele vs Gastroschisis: herniated viscera bowel+liver vs bowel only · sac present vs absent · associated anomalies common (50%) vs uncommon (<10%) · location umbilicus vs right of umbilicus · delivery vaginal/cesarean vs vaginal · Sx nonurgent vs urgent · prognostic factors associated anomalies (e.g. cardiac, chromosomal) vs condition of bowel
p8 pathophysiology: omphalocele failure of viscera to return to abdominal cavity · gastroschisis ventral body fold theory: failure of migration of lateral folds (more on right)
p9–10 prenatal Dx: omphalocele USG by GA 18 wk, ↑maternal AFP, multiple bowel loops (± liver) herniate into membrane-covered defect (not free-flowing) with cord inserting into omphalocele · gastroschisis USG by GA 20 wk or sooner, ↑maternal AFP, bowel loops freely floating in amniotic fluid + defect right of normal umbilical cord, IUGR
p11 initial management: IV access + fluid resuscitation · OG decompression · wrap bowel in warm saline-soaked gauze with non-evaporating wrap (plastic bag) · empirical ATB · thorough exam for intestinal atresia/associated anomalies
p12 Sx gastroschisis: immediate closure/primary repair · silo assisted/delayed closure with serial reduction over a few days (รูป)
p13 Sx omphalocele: immediate closure/primary repair · staged closure · escharification ("paint and wait") (รูป)
p14 agenda acute abdomen in children
p15 flow: abdominal pain → H&P (→ investigation if necessary) → localized pain / gut obstruction / peritonitis / with mass
p16 แผนภาพ DDx ปวดท้องตามตำแหน่ง (ภาษาไทย): RUQ ปวดชายโครงขวา = ท่อน้ำดีโป่งพอง (choledochal cyst), ถุงน้ำดีอักเสบ, นิ่วในถุงน้ำดี, เนื้องอกในตับ, ฝีในตับ, กรวยไตอักเสบขวา · epigastric ปวดลิ้นปี่ = ตับอ่อนอักเสบ, แผลในกระเพาะอาหาร, กระเพาะอาหารบิดพัน · LUQ ปวดชายโครงซ้าย = ฝีในม้าม, ม้ามบิดพัน, กรวยไตอักเสบซ้าย · periumbilical ปวดตรงกลางท้อง = ทางเดินอาหารซ้ำซ้อน (duplication), ลำไส้อักเสบ, ถุงน้ำ mesenteric, internal hernia, mesenteric lymphadenitis · RLQ ปวดท้องน้อยขวา = ไส้ติ่งอักเสบ, Meckel's diverticulitis, ปวดประจำเดือน, ไข่ตก (ovulation), ปีกมดลูกอักเสบ, เนื้องอกรังไข่บิดพัน, นิ่วในท่อไต · hypogastric ปวดท้องน้อยตรงกลาง = ไส้ติ่งอักเสบแตก, ปวดประจำเดือน, ไข่ตก · LLQ ปวดท้องน้อยซ้าย = ปวดประจำเดือน, ปีกมดลูกอักเสบ, ไข่ตก, เนื้องอกรังไข่บิดพัน, นิ่วในท่อไต, อุจจาระแข็ง (constipation)
p17 acute appendicitis: most common 8–15 yr (all ages) · extreme age (<5 yr) prone to rupture · presentation nonspecific abdominal pain, migratory pain to RLQ, fever, N/V, anorexia, diarrhea · PE tender RLQ (McBurney), guarding, fever · Ix leukocytosis shift to left, USG, CT (diameter > 6 mm) · Tx appendectomy open vs laparoscopic
p18 H&P suspected → CBC, UA ± BUN Cr Elyte, UPT if teenage female · Pediatric Appendicitis Score (cough/percussion/hopping tenderness RLQ 2, anorexia 1, fever 1, nausea/emesis 1, RLQ tenderness 2, migration 1, leukocytosis WBC ≥10,000 1, PMN neutrophilia 1; sum ≥6 suggests appendicitis) vs Alvarado (rebound 1, anorexia 1, fever >37.3 1, N/V 1, RLQ tenderness 2, migration 1, leukocytosis >10,000 2, left shift >75% 1; sum ≥7 suggests) · Low risk PAS (<4) low likelihood, imaging may not be warranted, consider other causes · Equivocal PAS (4–6) imaging helpful: ultrasound first study (reduce radiation); if not available/equivocal → CT abdomen/pelvis with IV contrast; surgical consult if equivocal scores and appendix not visualized · High risk PAS (>6) surgical consultation prior to or alongside imaging · Alvarado 1–4 discharge (~30% appendicitis), 5–6 observation/admission (~66%), 7–10 surgery (~93%)
p19 algorithm: H&P → labs → likely for appendicitis → appendectomy · uncertain → close observation / USG → normal appendix / not seen normal appendix / appendicitis(→ appendectomy) · not seen → CT → normal / not seen → close observation or diagnostic laparoscopy → appendicitis → appendectomy · normal → other diseases
p20 ruptured appendicitis: IV ATB + IV fluid → peritonitis without mass → appendectomy · peritonitis with RLQ mass → non-surgical → improved → interval appendectomy 6–8 wk after discharge · not improved within 24–48 hr → USG → appendiceal phlegmon (→ appendectomy) / appendiceal abscess (→ appendectomy / percutaneous drainage)
p21 Meckel's (Organ of TWO): ~2% of people, ~2 feet from ileocecal valve, ~2 inches long, 2× more common in males, often 2 types ectopic tissue (gastric/pancreatic) · presentation: diverticulitis, gut obstruction (intussusception, internal hernia from omphalomesenteric band), LGIB (ectopic gastric mucosa) · diagrams of vitelline remnants (Meckel, fibrous cord, umbilical sinus, vitelline cyst, umbilical fistula, vitelline artery)
p22 Tx: diverticulitis → diverticulectomy ± bowel resection · gut obstruction → laparotomy · LGIB → Meckel's scan and diverticulectomy or segmental bowel resection · Technetium-99m pertechnetate scan: most bleeding Meckel contain heterotopic gastric mucosa
p23 intussusception: most frequent cause of bowel obstruction in infants & toddlers (highest 4–9 months) · most well-nourished healthy infants, 2/3 boys · acquired invagination of proximal bowel (intussusceptum) into distal (intussuscipiens) · diagram ileocolic
p24 clinical: intermittent sudden cramping abdominal pain + currant jelly stool + palpable mass (รูปท้องมีลูกศร)
p25 plain AXR: dilated small bowel loops RLQ? (dilated loops of small bowel in the right lower quadrant) and RUQ soft tissue mass near hepatic flexure · US target sign · pseudokidney sign
p26 management non-operative: NG decompression, IV fluid, air contrast enema if no contraindications (free intraperitoneal air, peritonitis, hypotension refractory to resuscitation) · operative: unsuccessful/incomplete nonop reduction, signs of peritonitis, lead point, radiographic pneumoperitoneum
p27 agenda acute scrotum
p28 acute scrotum: torsion of testis · incarcerated/strangulated indirect inguinal hernia
p29 torsion typical < 3 yr or after puberty · presentation acute onset severe unilateral scrotal pain (sometimes on-off), swelling, erythema, N/V, urinary symptoms, fever (รูป)
p30 PE compare with contralateral: enlarged testis · absent cremasteric reflex (upward contraction of scrotum on stroking ipsilateral medial thigh; present does not exclude) · retracted upward · transverse orientation · negative Prehn's sign
p31 (infographic pedscases 2021) diagnosis primarily clinical; H&P often sufficient to bring straight to OR; U/A not indicated · colour Doppler US if Dx in question (↓perfusion, twisting of cord; limited in small prepubertal testes); DO NOT delay surgery for imaging if strongly suggestive · TWIST score: testicular swelling 2, hard testicle 2, absent cremasteric 1, N/V 1, high riding testis 1 → ≤2 low, 3–4 medium, ≥5 high · detorsion within 4–6 hr 97–100% viability, >12 hr 20–61%, >24 hr 0–14% · goal: OR within 6 hr of symptom onset · never delay surgery assuming nonviability from estimated duration
p32 surgical exploration: viable → surgical detorsion + orchiopexy (fixation) · not viable → orchiectomy · then contralateral exploration + orchiopexy (bell-clapper often bilateral) · recovery 1–2 wk · one functional testicle sufficient for normal fertility
